1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CANONICAL REUSE POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with the project integrity rules, E06 does NOT invent new model 
architectures, new preprocessing transformations, or arbitrary hyperparameter tuning.
All 5 models evaluated in E06 reuse the audited, validated implementations from 
the E05 benchmark and the verified NASA ablation repository codebase.

The 5 models selected for E06 Generalization are:
1. TE-Q-Transformer (Proposed Physics-Guided Quantum Transformer)
2. QNN-GRU (Quantum Neural Network Feature Map + GRU Baseline)
3. iTransformer (Inverted Variate Token Transformer, ICLR 2024 Spotlight)
4. Transformer (Classical Multi-Head Attention Encoder Baseline)
5. PatchTST (Channel-Independent Patch Transformer, ICLR 2023)

No auxiliary models (LSTM, GRU, CNN1D, TCN, DLinear, QLSTM, QGRU) are included, 
maintaining a laser focus on the contemporary Transformer and Quantum comparison suite.

3. PREPROCESSING & SPLIT INTEGRITY AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sequence Length: 512 points per discharge cycle (cycle-by-cycle windowing).
- Feature Order: [Voltage_V, Current_A, Temperature_C, Time_norm] (indices 0, 1, 2, 3).
- Target Definition: SOH = C_k / C_0 (normalized by cell initial capacity).
- Scaling Policy:
  * MinMaxScaler(feature_range=(0, 1)) fitted EXCLUSIVELY on training data.
  * Applied only to indices (0, 1, 3): Voltage, Current, and normalized Time.
  * Temperature (index 2) is strictly left in raw Celsius to preserve physical 
    kinetics in the Arrhenius gates (Ea_sei, Ea_pl).
  * No test data (B0018, B0032, B0053_test) is ever seen by the scaler.
  * No independent per-test-cell normalization or recalibration.
- Split Details:
  * Train Cells (660 cycles): B0005, B0006, B0007, B0029, B0030, B0031, 
    and B0053 first 70% (cycles 0–36, exactly 37 cycles).
  * Test Cells (187 cycles):
    - Unseen Cells (171 cycles): B0018 (132 cycles), B0032 (39 cycles).
    - Temporal Extrapolation (16 cycles): B0053 final 30% (cycles 37–52).
- Terminology Constraint:
  * E06 is NOT termed an "unseen-temperature" experiment.
  * The evaluation settings are strictly designated as:
    A. "unseen_cell" (B0018, B0032)
    B. "temporal_extrapolation" (B0053_test)

4. TRAINING PROTOCOL & HYPERPARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Common Optimizer: AdamW(lr=1e-3, weight_decay=1e-2)
- Loss Function: nn.MSELoss()
- Learning Rate Scheduler: ReduceLROnPlateau(mode="min", factor=0.5, patience=10)
- Gradient Clipping: nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(1.0)
- Maximum Epochs: 80
- Early Stopping: Patience = 20 epochs
- Checkpoint Criterion: Minimum training loss (strictly zero test-based tuning)
- Checkpoint Files: model_latest.pth and model_best.pth with full optimizer/scheduler state
- Batch Size: 8 for classical and hybrid models; 16 where beneficial for vectorized QNN-GRU.
